What is foxtail millet called in Telugu and Hindi?

Foxtail millet is korralu in Telugu, kangni in Hindi, thinai in Tamil and navane in Kannada. In Telugu and Hindi, proso millet is varigalu / chena, little millet samalu / kutki, kodo millet arikelu / kodo, barnyard millet udalu / sanwa, browntop millet andu korralu, pearl millet sajjalu / bajra, finger millet ragulu / ragi, and jowar jonnalu / jowar.
