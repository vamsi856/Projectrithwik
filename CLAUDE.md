# Rithwik website

Animated one-page site for **Rithwik Bird Feeds** and **Rithwik Industries** (Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh), a client project.
Live at https://vamsi856.github.io/Projectrithwik/ (GitHub Pages, served from the root of `main`).

## Layout

- `site/` is the source: an Astro 5 static site (GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, three.js for the 3D packs).
- The repo root (`index.html`, `_astro/`, `img/`, `birds/` …) is **generated**: `npm run build` builds `site/dist`
  and `site/scripts/publish.mjs` copies it to the root. Never edit root files by hand.
- One page, three views: the "book" cover, `#bird-feeds` and `#human-food` (Rithwik Industries).

## Commands (run in `site/`)

```
npm install
npm run dev                                   # http://localhost:4321/Projectrithwik/
npm run build                                 # builds and copies to the repo root
npx astro preview --port 4331                 # serve the build
node scripts/check-sizes.cjs                  # 13 widths x 3 views: no sideways spill, header OK
python3 scripts/make_photos.py                # photo sizes (pip install pillow pillow-heif)
```

Deploy = build, commit `site/` **and** the regenerated root files, push to `main`. Pages redeploys in about a minute.
If a push doesn't trigger Pages, push an empty commit.

## Where things are

- `site/src/data/products.ts`: every product, its variants (crop/colour options), photos, swatches, and `CONTACT`
  (phones, WhatsApp, emails, office and warehouse map links).
- `site/src/data/commons.json`: photo registry. Client photos have `own: true` and `local: 'photos/<key>.webp'`.
  Stock photos carry their Wikimedia credit, which the "Photo credits" panel lists.
- `site/src/data/photo-sizes.json`: generated by `make_photos.py`. Don't edit.
- `site/src/data/legacy.ts`: the family timeline 1925 to 2022 and `FOUNDERS` (round photo on the 2022 dot).
- `site/src/data/perches.ts`: the real birds that used to hop onto product cards (turned off).
- `site/src/scripts/main.ts`: page behaviour and animation (views, menus, paper birds, map tabs, forms, sound button).
  `flock.ts` is the hero macaw flock, `sound.ts` the birdsong and Human Food music, `pack3d.ts` the 3D packs.
- `site/src/styles/v2.css`: all styles. Components are in `site/src/components/`.

## Adding a client photo

1. Crop it from the original: 4:3, **grain only, no hands or background**, at least ~800px wide if possible.
2. Save it as `site/photo-src/<key>-rithwik.webp`.
3. Add `"<key>-rithwik": {"t": "<name>", "u": "", "w": W, "h": H, "p": "", "l": "", "a": "Rithwik", "local": "photos/<key>-rithwik.webp", "own": true}` to `commons.json`.
4. Point the product (`photo: '...'`) or a single variant (`{ label: 'Red', gst: '0%', photo: '...' }`) at it in `products.ts`.
5. Run `python3 scripts/make_photos.py`, then build. To replace a photo, delete `public/img/<key>-*` first
   (the script never overwrites). When a stock photo is no longer used, remove it from `commons.json`,
   `photo-sizes.json` and `public/img`, so the credits stay accurate.

## Client decisions (keep these)

- Phone for Call **and** WhatsApp everywhere: **92455 55556**. The number 83097 82770 was removed on purpose.
- Spelling: "Viraanch Exports and Imports" (2011 on the timeline).
- Bird Feeds hero: red scarlet macaws flying behind the pack (5 on desktop, 4 on phones).
- Scrolling: 3 gold folded-paper birds zigzag down Bird Feeds. They land on the founders' 2022 photo, take off,
  and land on the map pin at the end.
- Founders: Bysani Vasavi (left) and Bysani Sudheer (right), shown only as the big round photo on the 2022 dot, on both
  timelines. No macaw on the timeline, and no extra founders photos.
- Human Food sound is its original generated calm music only (tanpura-style drone, slow raga Mohanam notes, soft
  chimes, in `sound.ts`): no birdsong, no recorded flute, no click/whoosh effects. Bird Feeds is the recorded birdsong.
- Seeds (chia, quinoa, pumpkin, hulled sunflower, flax, basil) sit in Super foods, not Oil seeds.
- Names: "Sunflower seeds", "Safflower seeds". Ragi: "Ragi" on Human Food; on Bird Feeds "Finger millet" (option "Ragi"),
  right after Pearl millet.
- Foxtail has only Summer crop / Winter crop (no processed/unprocessed choice). Chia (Human Food) has Black and White.
- Each Human Food section opens with a cover cut by **wavy** slanted lines into one slice per product, each slice showing
  that product's photo (built from the products in `index.astro` with SVG masks; phones use two rows when a section has
  more than four).
- Bird Feeds "Who we are" shows the client's claim "No. 1 foxtail millet supplier in India": a 3D gold 1 (no "No."/"India" beside it) (CSS) that counts
  down from 50, then sways with a sheen and sparkles (paused off screen).
- Bird Feeds facts row (client's figures): Since 1925 · 20,000MT+ annual production · 3,000+ customers · 20+ range of products
  (GSTIN only in the footer on Bird Feeds). Numbers count up, no highlight bar.
- Certification seals show on both sites (owner's request; not under the Human Food hero), including ISO as a text seal: ISO's rules don't allow
  certified companies to use the ISO logo. No caption on the Bird Feeds certs photo.
- Vision section (both sites): an animated India map (official India outline, Natural Earth India view) of where we
  operate, from `site/src/data/network.ts`: Duvvur (main warehouse), Kurnool, Nandyal, Allagadda, Ballari, Challageri (Haveri dist.),
  Bangarpet, Hyderabad, Delhi, Neemuch, Rajkot, Kolkata. Map pins, no route lines; the Kurnool cluster is shown in a
  magnified circle. It replaced the 3D globe.
- Human Food facts row mirrors Bird Feeds (Since 1925 · 20,000MT+ · 3,000+ · 31+ range of products). Human Food copy
  says "retail packers, traders and bulk buyers".
- No real birds perched on the Bird Feeds cards (removed at the client's request; `SHOW_PERCHES` in ProductCard.astro).
- Human Food uses the original cream, ink and bronze colours: the dark forest-green theme was removed at the client's
  request (Nov 2026). Don't bring the green back.
- Bird Feeds "Who we are" is the client's text: processing unit, processed and unprocessed, traders, pet shops, bird
  keepers and export houses.
- Mission and "Our goal is simple" on both Vision sections are the client's own words (from Sudheer): keep them as written.
- Certification seals sit centred (a short row stays in the middle), cards in a row share one height.
- Quinoa: client-supplied Regular, Bold, Black and Red photos; Tricolor is still stock. Sesame Z-black and Chana White
  also use client-supplied photos (the Black sesame option keeps the credited stock photo).
- Product photos show the product only: **no bowls, jars or plates** (stock photos are cropped inside the pile).
- Never use watermarked stock previews (Bigstock, Shutterstock...) the client forwards: ask for the licensed file.
- The plant/warehouse photo section was removed on purpose. The warehouse is a map tab (Duvvur, 14.829389, 78.652194).
- Client grain photos show grain only: no hands, paper or background.
- Photos under ~760px wide are upscaled with OpenCV EDSR (x2/x4) before `make_photos.py`; keep the original crop too.
