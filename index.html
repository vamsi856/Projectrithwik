Who we are

Rithwik Bird Feeds is the bird feed side of the Rithwik family business in Kurnool.

We bring in millets, sunflower, jowar, maize and horse gram, clean and sort them at our Duvvur warehouse, and supply them raw or cleaned to traders, pet shops and bird keepers. Foxtail comes from both the summer and winter crops, and white proso goes through a Sortex colour sorter.
