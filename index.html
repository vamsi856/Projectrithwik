Who we are

Rithwik Bird Feeds carries forward a family grain trade that began in 1925.

We bring in seven millets, five kinds of jowar, maize, sunflower and safflower, clean and sort them at our Duvvur warehouse, and supply them raw or cleaned to traders, pet shops and bird keepers. Foxtail comes from both the summer and winter crops.

Since 1925

A century in the grain trade

The Bysani family has traded grain from this part of Andhra Pradesh for 101 years. Each generation built its own firm; Rithwik is the newest chapter.

  1. 1925 Bysani Govindappa Sathyanarayana & Co
  2. 1945 Bysani Satyanarayana Sathyanarayana & Co
  3. 1976 Bysani SubbaRaju Krishna Sai Traders
  4. 2011 Bysani Sudheer Vivaanch Exports and Imports
  5. 2022 Bysani Vasavi Rithwik Bird Feeds and Rithwik Industries
