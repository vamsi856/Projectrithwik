About Rithwik

A Kurnool family grain trade that began in 1925. Today it runs as two businesses: Rithwik Bird Feeds, India's No. 1 foxtail millet supplier, and Rithwik Industries, millets, super foods, pulses and spices for people.

Our story

  1. 1925Bysani Govindappa, Sathyanarayana & Co
  2. 1945Bysani Satyanarayana, Sathyanarayana & Co
  3. 1976Bysani SubbaRaju, Krishna Sai Traders
  4. 2011Bysani Sudheer, Viraanch Exports and Imports
  5. 2022Bysani Vasavi, Rithwik Bird Feeds and Rithwik Industries

Five generations of the Bysani family have traded grain from Kurnool. In 2022 Bysani Vasavi and Bysani Sudheer started Rithwik Bird Feeds and Rithwik Industries, carrying the trade forward with their own processing unit and a main warehouse at Duvvur.

Two businesses, one family

Mission

Our mission is to connect farmers directly with industries by sourcing quality agricultural produce, ensuring proper cleaning, grading, and processing, and delivering products that meet the specific requirements of every customer.

We aim to maintain high quality standards, build trusted partnerships, support farmers, and provide reliable agricultural solutions across different industries.

Our goal is simple: Empower farmers, ensure quality, and make the right products reach the right industries in the right way.

Where we operate

Main warehouse at Duvvur, and operations in Kurnool, Nandyal, Allagadda, Bangarpet, Ballari, Challageri, Hyderabad, Delhi, Neemuch, Rajkot and Kolkata. We deliver across India.
